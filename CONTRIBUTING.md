# 🍵 Spilling the Tea (Contributing Guidelines)

First of all, thank you for considering a contribution!  
Whether you’re fixing a typo, adding a translation, or suggesting a new blend, your input makes the Tea License warmer and more wonderful.

## ☕ Code of Conduct
We steep together in the **Tea House Code** (see [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)).  
Be kind, be respectful, and never scald anyone. If you can’t say anything nice, have a sip of tea first.

## 🫖 How to Contribute

### 1. Reporting a Cracking Teapot (Bug)
If you spot a broken link, a missing comma, or a loophole that leaves a bitter taste:
- Open a new issue.
- Give it a clear title, like “Clause 4 lacks biscuit policy”.
- Describe what you expected vs. what you got.

### 2. Suggesting a New Leaf (Enhancement)
Got an idea for a new clause, a translation, or a tea-themed badge variation?
- Start a discussion in the Issues tab.
- Tag it with `enhancement` or `translation`.
- We’ll brew on it together before any code (or text) is poured.

### 3. Pull Request Process
1. **Fork** the repository and clone your fork.
2. Create a new branch with a tea‑riffic name: `add/oolong-translation`, `fix/biscuit-typo`, etc.
3. Make your changes. For translations, place the file in `translations/` named `LICENSE-[language code].md` (e.g., `LICENSE-fr.md` for French).
4. Update the `README.md` if you add a translation or a new badge variant.
5. Open a Pull Request to the `main` branch.
   - Describe what you changed and why it makes the tea taste better.
   - Mention any related issues.
6. A maintainer (the Tea Master) will review it. We may ask for a small tweak, like adding a sugar cube of clarity.

### 4. Translation Guidelines
- Keep the playful, warm tone intact. Tea puns may vary by language, but the spirit must remain.
- Preserve the original clause numbers and structure.
- Include the same copyright notice at the top, substituting the translation note for the original language.
- Add your name (or handle) as the translator in a comment at the top of the file.

## ✍️ Style Guide
- Write in plain, sippable English (or your target language).
- Use tea puns sparingly, but enough to make people smile — not groan.
- Maintain the original line breaks and spacing so diffs stay clean.
- Always use “Tea” with a capital T when referring to the licensed work.

## 🍪 A Note on Licensing
By contributing, you agree that your contribution will be licensed under the same Tea License as the rest of the repository. Your words will steep into the communal pot for everyone to enjoy.

Now go ahead, warm the pot, and pour in your ideas. We can’t wait to taste them!
