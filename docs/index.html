🍵 Tea License 2.0

Permissive, protective, and one of a kind.

The Tea License 2.0 is a unique open-source software license that blends the permissiveness of MIT with a set of carefully balanced legal mechanisms: mandatory UI attribution, weak copyleft on source modifications, patent peace, ethical use restrictions, moral rights protection, a symbolic biscuit covenant, and mandatory tea mediation before litigation. It’s designed for creators who want broad sharing without giving up influence over how their work is used.

Key Features

  • Permissive Core – Use, modify, distribute, and sublicense commercially.
  • Visible Credit – Attribution must appear in user interfaces, not just in a LICENSE file.
  • Weak Copyleft on Source – Modifications stay Tea-licensed, but binaries can be combined with proprietary code.
  • Patent Peace – Any patent lawsuit by a licensee against a contributor terminates all rights.
  • Ethical Boundaries – Use for mass surveillance, autonomous weapons, or new fossil fuel extraction is prohibited.
  • Moral Rights – Prohibits uses that harm the creator’s reputation (hate speech, disinformation).
  • The Biscuit Covenant – Upon request, licensee sends a biscuit or donates $1 to charity once a year.
  • Tea Mediation – All disputes must first go through a mediation where both parties share a cup of tea.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Tea License 2.0 legally enforceable?

Yes. It is a copyright license with clear conditions. The ethical restrictions, patent clause, and mediation clause are contractual conditions that can be enforced in court. The biscuit covenant is a valid consideration under contract law. As with any license, you should consult a qualified attorney if you need legal advice for your specific situation.

Why include an ethical use restriction?

To give creators the ability to say their work will not be used for mass surveillance, lethal autonomous weapons, or new fossil fuel projects. If a licensee breaches this, their rights terminate automatically. This is a material condition of the license, not a mere statement of values.

What is the Biscuit Covenant and is it serious?

Yes, it is a serious, enforceable clause. Upon written request (once per year), the licensee must either send a biscuit to the licensor or donate $1 to a charity. This provides consideration, making the license a bilateral contract rather than a bare permission. The token nature doesn't diminish its legal force.

Do I really have to mediate over a cup of tea?

Yes. Before any court action, the parties must attempt mediation in good faith and share a cup of tea (or a beverage of choice) as a symbolic gesture. If mediation fails after 60 days, litigation may proceed. This is a condition precedent under the license.

How is the patent clause different from Apache 2.0?

Apache 2.0 terminates patent licenses only if you sue over the specific licensed work. Tea License 2.0 terminates all rights if you file any patent lawsuit against any contributor, regardless of the subject matter. This creates a stronger deterrent against patent aggression.

Can I combine Tea 2.0 code with proprietary software?

Yes. The weak copyleft applies only to the source code of the Tea-licensed portion itself. You can link it with proprietary code and distribute the combined binary under your own license, provided the Tea-licensed source (and modifications) remain available under Tea 2.0.
