Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Tea License legally binding?

It’s a real copyright license with clear conditions, much like the MIT License but with more biscuit encouragement. While its wording is playful, the legal intent is serious. For high‑stakes legal assurance, consult a lawyer who appreciates a good cuppa.

Can I use Tea‑licensed work commercially?

Yes! You can sell, modify, and build businesses on Tea‑licensed code or art. Just give credit, keep the license notice, and you’re golden—no royalties, no bitter strings attached.

What does “Leave the tag on” mean exactly?

Keep the original license text and copyright notice with the work. If you create a website or app, you can put it in a LICENSE file or mention “Tea License” in your documentation. The tag just lets others know where the tea came from.

Do I really have to offer biscuits?

Strictly speaking, no—but it’s strongly encouraged. The biscuit clause is a friendly reminder that sharing is sweeter together. No one has ever been sued for a lack of biscuits… yet.

Can I mix Tea License with GPL or MIT code?

Absolutely. The Tea License is permissive, so you can combine it with other open‑source code. Just make sure you respect the other license’s terms as well, and keep the Tea notice visible somewhere.

How is the Tea License different from the MIT License?

Both are permissive. The Tea License has the exact same freedom to use, modify, and distribute. The difference? Tea adds warmth, a dash of whimsy, and a biscuit recommendation. Legally, they are very similar.
