# 🍵 Tea License 2.0

The **Tea License 2.0** is a unique open-source software license that combines the permissiveness of MIT with a set of distinctive legal mechanisms not found together in any other license. It is designed for creators who want broad sharing while enforcing ethical boundaries, patent peace, visible attribution, weak copyleft on source, moral rights protection, and a symbolic token of appreciation—all resolved over a cup of tea when disagreements arise.

## Key Features

- **Permissive Core** – Use, modify, distribute, and sublicense freely, commercially or otherwise.
- **Mandatory UI Attribution** – Your credit must appear prominently in user interfaces, not just buried in a LICENSE file.
- **Weak Copyleft on Source** – Changes to the licensed code itself must stay under the Tea License; however, you may combine it with proprietary code and distribute the binary under your own terms (similar to MPL 2.0).
- **Patent Peace** – Any patent litigation by a licensee against any contributor terminates all rights immediately—broader than Apache 2.0's retaliation.
- **Ethical Use Restriction** – Use for mass surveillance, autonomous weapons, or new fossil fuel extraction is a material breach and terminates the license.
- **Moral Rights Protection** – The software may not be used in ways that damage the author’s reputation (e.g., hate speech, disinformation), with a right to cure.
- **The Biscuit Covenant** – A real, enforceable peppercorn: upon request, licensees must send the licensor a biscuit or make a $1 charity donation once per year.
- **Mandatory Tea Mediation** – All disputes must first go through a mediation session where both parties share a cup of tea, before any court action.
- **Disclaimer & Termination** – Standard no-warranty, and automatic termination for uncured material breaches.

## How to Use

1. Copy the [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) file into your project’s root directory.
2. Replace `[Year]` and `[Licensor Name]` with the appropriate year and your name or organization.
3. Add the badge below to your `README.md` (or any documentation) to let others know you use Tea License 2.0.
4. (Optional) Include a comment
5. header in your source files: // Copyright (c) 2026 YourName - Tea License 2.0 (see LICENSE)
